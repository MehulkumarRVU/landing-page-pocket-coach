Great coaching shouldn't be a privilege.

Professional coaching can be expensive, far away and hard to get consistently. Many athletes practise alone, with no one to tell them what their technique, movement or performance actually looks like.

  • Cost

    Personalised coaching may be unaffordable for beginners and grassroots athletes.

  • Distance

    Athletes in smaller cities, towns and rural areas may have limited access to qualified coaches.

  • No feedback

    Practising alone makes it hard to spot technical mistakes.

  • No record

    Athletes need a consistent way to review performance and see improvement.

Training without limitations.

Technology should make sport more accessible, not less. PocketCoach includes adaptive training configurations for different physical abilities and training setups.

  • Standing-based training
  • Seated training
  • Wheelchair-based training
  • Accessibility and movement preferences
  • Adaptive assessment for supported movements
  • Configurable camera orientation and equipment

Adaptive feedback depends on the supported exercise, configuration and available visual evidence. PocketCoach does not diagnose, treat or replace medical or rehabilitation professionals.
